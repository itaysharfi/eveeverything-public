Our Mission

Eve is on a mission to transform women's health by making healing more accessible, empowering, and deeply restorative.

Through a client-centered approach, trusted collaborations with healthcare providers, and the latest evidence-based tools for nervous system regulation and stress reduction, we help women regain energy, balance, and peace — making the path to optimal health clear, supported, and sustainable.

Holistic Approach

We believe that when the body, mind, and nervous system are aligned, the body functions at its best. In this optimal state, chronic pain can ease, autoimmune symptoms may improve, gut health is restored, and fertility can return naturally.

That's why we take time to understand the whole person — so we can craft a healing plan that truly fits you.

"Research shows that stress, lifestyle habits, and nutrition play a vital role in reproductive health. It's time we pay closer attention. True healing doesn't come from treating symptoms alone — it comes from caring for the whole person. That's how we give our clients the best possible chance."

— Heather Sharfi, PhD

Meet the Founder

Heather founded EVE after years of witnessing the lack of comprehensive care for women's health — both as a scientist and as a woman. As a scientist, she noticed that less funding is allocated to women's conditions, and most research targets the average male. As a woman, she experienced firsthand the inadequate support offered when facing fertility-related issues.

With a background in cancer biochemistry, Heather brings together modern science and holistic practices to help women reconnect with their bodies, restore hormonal balance, and live fuller, more aligned lives.

B.Sc. Biology M.Sc. Cancer Metabolism PhD Cancer DNA Damage Response

"I want to make the journey to wellness an empowering one! After all, there is truly no greater honor than helping women create new life and achieve their dreams of parenthood and health."

Eve Services

We provide the knowledge needed to make your next decision, recommend the treatment that is just what you need, and offer emotional support along the way.

Fertility Consultation

Personalized guidance on your fertility journey. We review your health history, recommend lifestyle adjustments, and connect you with top specialists in your area.

Women's Health Coaching

Holistic coaching that addresses the whole person — physical, emotional, social, and spiritual well-being. Evidence-based tools customized to your unique needs.

Hormonal Balancing

Data-driven, scientifically-backed approaches to restoring hormonal balance. We address root causes using nutrition, stress management, and anti-inflammatory lifestyle strategies.

Somatic Work & Teachings

Techniques including dream work, guided meditation, imagery therapy, and breathwork to help you reconnect with your body and regulate your nervous system.

Eve Academy

Educational programs and workshops designed to empower you with knowledge about your body, health, and wellness.

The Women's Wellness Club

A supportive community space for women to learn, share, and grow together on their wellness journeys.
