Women's Wellness Club

Nervous System Regulation
for Resilience

A weekly in-person workshop guiding you through the art and science of nervous system regulation. Through somatic practices, breathwork, and guided meditation, learn to shift from survival mode into vitality, presence, and deep wellness.

Reserve Your Spot

What You'll Experience

Nervous System Regulation

Shift your nervous system from survival mode into a state of calm, presence, and vitality. Activate your body's natural rest-and-restore response.

Release Stored Tension

Gentle somatic practices to unwind stress and tension that has accumulated in your body over time, allowing deep physical and emotional release.

Emotional Resilience

Build the capacity to navigate life's challenges with greater ease, clarity, and emotional balance.

Better Sleep & Digestion

Calm an overactive nervous system for deeper rest, improved gut health, and stronger immune function.

Hormonal Balance

Support your body's natural hormonal equilibrium through regulation techniques that reduce chronic stress and inflammation.

Mind-Body Reconnection

Restore trust in your body's innate wisdom and healing capacity. Reconnect with yourself on a deeper level.

Meet Your Guide

Heather Sharfi, PhD, founded EVE after years of witnessing the lack of comprehensive care for women's health. With a background in cancer biochemistry, she brings together modern science and holistic practices to help women reconnect with their bodies and restore balance.

Through somatic work, breathwork, and nervous system regulation techniques, Heather guides women toward lasting wellness and emotional resilience.
